# Travertine Task Guide Test Matrix

## Normal numeric setup

- Finish: tumbled, sandblasted, textured or smooth
- Filled: unfilled
- Sealed: unsealed
- Joints: sound grout or sound mortar
- Area: outdoor paving
- Result: 800 PSI editorial starting target, 600–1000 PSI working range

## Conservative sensitive setup

- Honed, filled, partially filled, sealed or pool-surround
- Requires the sound-surface confirmation checkbox
- Result: 600 PSI editorial starting target, 600–800 PSI working range
- Overall status: Compatible with caution

## Calculation blocked

- Polished finish
- Indoor installation
- Wall or roof installation
- Unknown finish, filled status, sealer status or joint condition
- Loose or missing joints
- Sensitive variant without sound-surface confirmation

## Additional cautions

- Jointing sand: calculation may proceed, but warns about sand displacement
- Surface cleaner: calculation may proceed, but warns that lance guidance does not transfer directly because the jets operate closer to the stone
- Pressure below the editorial range is treated as conservative rather than as exceeding the surface limit; the result warns that cleaning effectiveness may be reduced
